Have I Been Scammed?

If you're worried about a possible scam, you're not alone — we're here to support you.

You can:

  • 📝 Share your story
  • 📷 Upload a screenshot of the ad or conversation
  • 🌐 Provide the website address
  • 🎤 Upload an audio recording

We'll review the information, give you an evaluation, share general advice, and provide contact details for law enforcement.

If it looks like you've been scammed, we can also help you draft a clear report to send to the authorities.
